# Privacy Policy & Data Collection Notice

_Last Updated: August 2026 · Effective Immediately for all Encircled web services and Desktop Companion applications._

---

### Core Principles

- **Zero Process Memory Access**  
  We never read, inject, or change game memory. We only parse autosaves explicitly generated by Hearts of Iron IV on your disk.

- **100% Transparent Telemetry**  
  Every casualty, equipment count, and factory metric processed by our engine is visibly displayed in your match summary.

- **No Data Monetization**  
  We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or gameplay statistics to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

---

### 01. Information We Collect

Encircled operates on the principle of strict data minimization. We only collect the information necessary to provide real-time match telemetry, competitive matchmaking, and anti-cheat verification:

- **A. Account Credentials & Identification**  
  When you register, we store your chosen username, email address, and cryptographically salted password hash (never stored in plaintext).

- **B. Gameplay Telemetry & Save Artifacts**  
  When a match is hosted or a save file is uploaded via the Desktop Companion or Web Reader, our parser extracts in-game data: country casualties, military/civilian factory counts, equipment attrition curves, active war participants, in-game calendar timestamps, and game rule settings. We do not read or upload any non-HOI4 files from your machine.

- **C. Technical & Process Integrity Logs**  
  To guarantee competitive fairness in Ranked lobbies, the companion app and server record SHA-256 save file hashes (for replay detection), connection timestamps, rate-limiting IP logs, and binary runtime checks confirming the absence of developer console (`-debug`) flags.

---

### 02. How We Use Your Information

- **Live Multi-Device Telemetry:** Broadcasting active match casualty rates and industrial metrics to lobby participants and spectators via secure WebSockets (SignalR).
- **Competitive MMR & Leaderboard Ranking:** Computing role-weighted ratings, tier ranks (Bronze through Field Marshal), and career match histories.
- **Anti-Cheat & Save Integrity Enforcement:** Preventing timeline rollbacks, duplicate save replay attacks, and unhistorical mode spoofing in verified ranked matches.
- **Platform Security & Abuse Prevention:** Enforcing rate limits on authentication endpoints and preventing malicious DDoS floods.

---

### 03. Data Retention & Automatic Expiration

- **Active Lobby Listings:** Completed multiplayer lobby listings on the public browser are automatically expired from the active feed after 24 hours.
- **Career Records & Match Summaries:** Underlying match telemetry JSON and MMR leaderboards are preserved permanently to maintain historical record integrity and player progression.
- **Authentication Sessions:** Short-lived JWT tokens expire after 30 minutes. Secure 7-day refresh tokens expire automatically upon inactivity or explicit logout.

---

### 04. Encircled Desktop Companion Security

> **Filesystem Watcher Security Guarantee**  
> The Encircled Desktop Companion operates solely as an external file system observer. It uses standard OS-level directory notification APIs (`ReadDirectoryChangesW`) to detect when Hearts of Iron IV writes an autosave file to your user Documents folder.
>
> The companion does **not** inject code, does **not** change game files, does **not** read web browsing history or personal files, and will never trigger Valve Anti-Cheat (VAC) or Paradox multiplayer bans.
